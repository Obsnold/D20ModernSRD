# Improved Brawl

## Prerequisites

Brawl, base attack bonus +3.

## Benefit

When making an unarmed attack, the character receives a +2
competence bonus on his or her attack roll, and the character deals
nonlethal damage equal to 1d8 + the character’s Strength modifier.

## Normal

Unarmed attacks normally deal nonlethal damage equal to
1d3 + Strength modifier.
