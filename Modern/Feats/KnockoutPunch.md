# Knockout Punch

## Prerequisites

Brawl, base attack bonus +3.

## Benefit

When making the character’s first unarmed attack against a
flat-footed opponent, treat a successful attack as a critical hit. This
damage is nonlethal damage.

## Special

Even if the character has the ability to treat unarmed
damage as lethal damage, the damage from a knockout punch is always
nonlethal.
