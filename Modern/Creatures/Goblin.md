# Goblin

## Species Traits

**Skill Bonus:** Goblins gain a +4 species bonus on Move Silently
checks.

**Bonus Feat:** Goblins are keenly aware of their surroundings and
receive the bonus feat Alertness.

Automatic Language: Goblins read, write, and speak Goblin.
