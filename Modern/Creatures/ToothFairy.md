## Tooth Fairy

**Species Traits**

**Fey Rod (Sp):** A tooth fairy’s preferred weapon is a 1-foot-long rod
of ash wood. A fey rod allows its wielder to cast the following spells
as a 3rd-level Mage (save DC 10 + spell level): daze (DC 10), knock,
light, magic missile, and sleep (DC 11). Using any of the rod’s powers
is an attack action. The fey rod can cast seven spells per day and has a
hardness of 5, 1 hit point, and a break DC of 11. A tooth fairy that
loses its fey rod fights to the death to retrieve it or takes 1d4 points
of temporary Wisdom damage every 24 hours the fairy and the rod remain
separated. Destroying the rod instantly slays the tooth fairy, turning
both to dust.

**Invisibility (Sp):** A tooth fairy can turn itself invisible at will,
as a move action. This ability works only after sunset and before
sunrise, but the duration of the invisibility is otherwise unlimited. An
invisible tooth fairy becomes visible when it attacks.

**Anyspeak (Sp):** A tooth fairy can speak, read, and write any spoken
language.

Bonus Feat: Tooth fairies gain the bonus feat Simple Weapons
Proficiency.
