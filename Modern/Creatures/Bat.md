# Bat

## Species Traits

**Blindsight (Ex):** Bats can “see” by emitting high-frequency sounds,
inaudible to most other creatures, that allow them to locate objects and
creatures within 30 feet. Ultrasonic noise forces the bat to rely on its
weak vision, which has a maximum range of 5 feet.

**Skill Bonuses:** Bats receive a +4 species bonus on Listen and Spot
checks. These bonuses are lost if the bat’s blindsight is negated.
