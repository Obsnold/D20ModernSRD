# Kobold

## Species Traits

**Light Sensitivity (Ex):** Kobolds take a –1 penalty on attack rolls in
sunlight or in brightly lit areas.

**Skill Bonuses:** Kobolds receive a +2 species bonus on Craft
(mechanical) and Search checks.

**Bonus Feat:** Kobolds are keenly aware of their surroundings and
receive the bonus feat Alertness.

**Automatic Language:** Kobolds read, write, and speak Draconic.
