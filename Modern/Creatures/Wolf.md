# Wolf

## Species Traits

**Scent (Ex):** This ability allows a wolf to detect approaching
enemies, sniff out hidden foes, and track by sense of smell. See Special
Qualities for more information.

**Trip (Ex):** A wolf that hits with a bite attack can attempt to trip
the opponent as a free action (see page 152) without making a touch
attack or provoking an attack of opportunity. If the attempt fails, the
opponent cannot react to trip the wolf.

**Skill Bonuses:** Wolves receive a +4 species bonus on Survival checks
when tracking by scent.
