# Crocodile

## Species Traits

**Aquatic:** Crocodiles can move in water without making Swim checks and
cannot drown in water.

**Improved Grab (Ex):** To use this ability, a crocodile must hit an
opponent of its size or smaller with its bite attack. If it gets a hold,
the crocodile grabs the opponent with its mouth and drags it into deep
water, attempting to pin it to the bottom.

**Skill Bonus:** Crocodiles gain a +12 species bonus on Hide checks when
submerged.
