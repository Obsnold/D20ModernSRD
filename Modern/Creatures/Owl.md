# Owl

## Species Traits

Skill Bonuses: Owls receive a +8 species bonus on Listen checks and a
+14 species bonus on Move Silently checks. They receive a +8 species
bonus on Spot checks in dusk and darkness.

Bonus Feat: Owls gain the bonus feat Weapon Finesse (claw).
