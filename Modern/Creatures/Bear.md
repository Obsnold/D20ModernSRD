
## Bear

**Species Traits**

Bears have the following traits.

**Improved Grab (Ex):** To use this ability, the bear must hit with a
claw attack.

**Scent (Ex):** This ability allows a bear to detect approaching
enemies, sniff out hidden foes, and track by sense of smell.

**Bear:** CR 4; Large animal; HD 6d8+24; hp 51; Mas 19; Init +1; Spd 40
ft.; Defense 15, touch 10, flat-footed 14 (–1 size, +1 Dex, +5 natural);
BAB +4; Grap +16; Atk +11 melee (1d8+8, claw); Full Atk +11 melee
(1d8+8, 2 claws), +6 melee (2d8+4 bite); FS 10 ft. by 10 ft.; Reach 10
ft.; SQ improved grab, low-light vision, scent; AL none; SV Fort +9, Ref
+6, Will +3; AP 0; Rep +0; Str 27, Dex 13, Con 19, Int 2, Wis 12, Cha 6.

**Skills:** Climb +18, Listen +6, Spot +6.

**Feats:** None.

**Advancement:** 7–10 HD (Large).
