# Bear

## Species Traits

Bears have the following traits.

**Improved Grab (Ex):** To use this ability, the bear must hit with a
claw attack.

**Scent (Ex):** This ability allows a bear to detect approaching
enemies, sniff out hidden foes, and track by sense of smell.
