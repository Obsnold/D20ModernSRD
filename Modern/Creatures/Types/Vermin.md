# Vermin

This type includes insects, arachnids, other arthropods, worms, and
similar invertebrates. See Table: Vermin for physical ability scores,
recommended minimum Hit Dice, and damage based on size.

| Stat               | Value                                                                     |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Hit Die            | d8                                                                        |
| Base Attack Bonus  | 3/4 of total Hit Dice (see Table: Creature Saves and Base Attack Bonuses) |
| Good Saving Throws | Fortitude                                                                 |
| Skill Points       | 10–15                                                                     |
| Feats              | None                                                                      |

## Traits

Vermin share the following additional traits.

**Weapon and Armor Proficiency:** Vermin are proficient with their
natural weapons only. They are not proficient with armor.

**Ability Scores:** Vermin have no Intelligence score.

**Potent Venom:** Medium-size or larger poisonous vermin get a bonus to
the save DC of their poison based on their size, as follows: Medium-size
+2, Large +4, Huge +6, Gargantuan +8, Colossal +10.

**Darkvision (Ex):** Most vermin with visual sensory organs have
darkvision with a range of 60 feet.

**Blindsight (Ex):** Most vermin without visual sensory organs have
blindsight with a range of 60 feet.

**Immunities:** Vermin are immune to mind-affecting effects.

**Resistance to Massive Damage (Ex):** Vermin gain a +5 species bonus on
Fortitude saves to negate the effects of massive damage.
