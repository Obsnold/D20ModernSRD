# Cat

## Species Traits

**Skill Bonuses:** Cats receive a +8 species bonus on Balance checks and
a +4 species bonus on Hide and Move Silently checks. In areas of tall
grass or heavy undergrowth, the cat’s Hide bonus rises to +8. Cats use
their Dexterity modifier for Climb checks.

**Bonus Feats:** A cat gains the bonus feats Weapon Finesse (bite) and
Weapon Finesse (claw).
