# Herd Animal

## Species Traits

**Stampede (Ex):** A frightened herd flees as a group in a random
direction, but always away from the perceived source of danger. It runs
over anything of size Large or smaller that gets in its way, dealing
1d12 points of damage for every five animals in the herd. A successful
Reflex save (DC 16) halves the damage.
