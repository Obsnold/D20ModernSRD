# Shark

## Species Traits

**Aquatic:** Sharks can move in water without making Swim checks and
cannot drown in water.

**Keen Scent (Ex):** A shark can notice creatures by scent in a 180-foot
radius and detect blood in the water at ranges of up to one mile.

**Bonus Feat:** Sharks gain the bonus feat Weapon Finesse (bite).
