# Battle Mind Powers

## 0-Level Battle Mind Powers

 - [Detect Psionics (Wis)](Psionics/DetectPsionics.md): Manifester can detect the presence of psionic activity.

 - [Far Punch (Con)](Psionics/FarPunch.md): Telekinetic strike deals 1 damage.

 - [Finger of Fire (Int)](Psionics/FingerOfFire.md): Deal 1d3 fire damage to target.

 - [Valor (Str)](Psionics/Valor.md): Gain a +1 morale bonus on saving throws.

 - [Verve (Str)](Psionics/Verve.md): Gain 1 temporary hit point.

## 1st-Level Battle Mind Powers

 - [Combat Precognition (Wis)](Psionics/CombatPrecognition.md): Gain a +1 insight bonus to Defense.

 - [Fire Bolt (Int)](Psionics/FireBolt.md): Deals 1d6+1 fire damage to target.

 - [Lesser Bioweapon (Str)](Psionics/LesserBioweapon.md): Create a staff of bioenergy that deals 1d4 bludgeoning damage.

 - [Lesser Concussion (Con)](Psionics/LesserConcussion.md): Mentally pummel target for 1d6 damage.

 - [Vigor (Str)](Psionics/Vigor.md): Gain 3 temporary hit points.

## 2nd-Level Battle Mind Powers

 - [Claws of the Bear (Str)](Psionics/ClawsOfTheBear.md): Manifester’s claw attack deals 1d12 damage.

 - [Combat Focus (Wis)](Psionics/CombatFocus.md): Gain a +4 insight bonus to initiative.

 - [Combat Prescience (Wis)](Psionics/CombatPrescience.md): Gain a +2 insight bonus on attack rolls.

 - [Concussion (Con)](Psionics/Concussion.md): Mentally pummel target for 3d6 damage.

 - [Darkvision (Wis)](Psionics/Darkvision.md): See in the dark.

 - [Electric Charge (Int)](Psionics/ElectricCharge.md): Shocking touch deals 2d6 damage to target.

## 3rd-Level Battle Mind Powers

 - [Lightning Strike (Int)](Psionics/LightningStrike.md): Deals 3d6 electrical damage in a 30-foot radius.

 - [Metaphysical Weapon (Int)](Psionics/MetaphysicalWeapon.md): Weapon gains a +3 enhancement bonus.

 - [Mind Darts (Int)](Psionics/MindDarts.md): A flurry of mental bursts deals 5d6 damage to target.

 - [Negate Psionics (Con)](Psionics/NegatePsionics.md): Cancels psionic powers and effects.

 - [Whitefire (Int)](Psionics/Whitefire.md): Deals 5d4 fire damage in 20-foot radius.

## 4th-Level Battle Mind Powers

 - [Fire Storm (Int)](Psionics/FireStorm.md): Deals 5d6 fire damage in 30-foot radius.

 - [Greater Bioweapon (Str)](Psionics/GreaterBioweapon.md): Creates a staff of bioenergy that deals 2d8 bludgeoning damage.

 - [Natural Armor (Str)](Psionics/NaturalArmor.md): Manifester gains a +4 natural armor bonus to Defense.

 - [Psychofeedback (Str)](Psionics/Psychofeedback.md): Use power points to boost Str, Dex, and/or Con modifiers.
