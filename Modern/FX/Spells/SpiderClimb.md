# Spider Climb

| Stat             | Value                   |
|------------------|-------------------------|
| School           | Transmutation           |
| Level            | Acolyte 2, Mage 2       |
| Components       | V, S, M                 |
| Casting Time     | Attack action           |
| Range            | Touch                   |
| Target           | Creature touched        |
| Duration         | 10 minutes/level        |
| Saving Throw     | Will negates (harmless) |
| Spell Resistance | Yes (harmless)          |

The subject can climb and travel on vertical surfaces or even traverse
ceilings. The affected creature must have its hands free to climb in
this manner. The subject gains a climb speed of 20 feet and need not
make Climb checks to scale a surface.
