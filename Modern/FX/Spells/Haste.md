# Haste

| Stat             | Value                           |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------- |
| School           | Transmutation                   |
| Level            | Mage 3                          |
| Components       | V, S, M                         |
| Casting Time     | Attack action                   |
| Range            | Close (25 ft. + 5 ft./2 levels) |
| Target           | One creature                    |
| Duration         | 1 round/level                   |
| Saving Throw     | Fortitude negates (harmless)    |
| Spell Resistance | Yes (harmless)                  |

The transmuted creature moves and acts more quickly than normal. This
extra speed has several effects.

When making a full attack action, the subject may make one extra attack
with any weapon he or she is holding. The attack is made using the
character’s full base attack bonus, plus any modifiers appropriate to
the situation. (This benefit does not actually grant an extra action, so
a character can’t use it to cast a second spell or otherwise take an
extra action in the round.)

The subject gains a +2 dodge bonus to Defense. Any condition that makes
the subject lose his or her Dexterity bonus to Defense (if any) also
makes the subject lose dodge bonuses.

All of the subject’s modes of movement (including normal movement,
burrow, climb, fly, and swim) increase by 30 feet (to a maximum of
double the subject’s normal speed).

Haste dispels and counters slow.
