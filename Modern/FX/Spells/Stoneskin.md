# Stoneskin

| Stat             | Value                                |
|------------------|--------------------------------------|
| School           | Abjuration                           |
| Level            | Mage 4                               |
| Components       | V, S, M                              |
| Casting Time     | Attack action                        |
| Range            | Touch                                |
| Target           | Creature touched                     |
| Duration         | 10 minutes/level or until discharged |
| Saving Throw     | Will negates (harmless)              |
| Spell Resistance | Yes (harmless)                       |

The subject gains damage reduction 10/— against physical attacks. Once
the spell has prevented a total of 10 points of damage per caster level,
it is discharged.

**Material Components:** Purchase DC 23.
