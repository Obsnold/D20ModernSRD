# Searing Light

| Stat | Value |
|------------------|---------------------------------|
| School | Evocation |
| Level | Acolyte 3 |
| Components | V, S |
| Casting Time | Attack action |
| Range | Medium (100 ft. + 10 ft./level) |
| Effect | Ray |
| Duration | Instantaneous |
| Saving Throw | None |
| Spell Resistance | Yes |

The caster must succeed at a ranged touch attack to strike his or her
target. A creature struck by this ray of light takes 1d8 points of
damage per two caster levels (maximum 5d8). Undead creatures take 1d6
points of damage per caster level (maximum 10d6), and undead creatures
particularly vulnerable to sunlight take 1d8 points of damage per caster
level (maximum 10d8). Constructs and inanimate objects take only 1d6
points of damage per two caster levels (maximum 5d6).
