# Inflict Moderate Wounds

| Stat             | Value            |
| ---------------- | ---------------- |
| School           | Necromancy       |
| Level            | Acolyte 2        |
| Components       | V, S             |
| Casting Time     | Attack action    |
| Range            | Touch            |
| Target           | Creature touched |
| Duration         | Instantaneous    |
| Saving Throw     | Will half        |
| Spell Resistance | Yes              |

The caster lays his or her hand upon a creature and channels negative
energy that deals 2d8 points of damage +1 point per caster level
(maximum +10).

Since undead are powered by negative energy, this spell cures them of a
like amount of damage rather than harming them.
