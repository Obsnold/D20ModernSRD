# Mass Inflict Light Wounds

| Stat             | Value                                                             |
|------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------|
| School           | Necromancy                                                        |
| Level            | Acolyte 5                                                         |
| Components       | V, S                                                              |
| Casting Time     | Attack action                                                     |
| Range            | Close (25 ft. + 5 ft./2 levels)                                   |
| Target           | One creature/level, no two of which can be more than 30 ft. apart |
| Duration         | Instantaneous                                                     |
| Saving Throw     | Will half (harmless)                                              |
| Spell Resistance | Yes (harmless)                                                    |

Negative energy spreads out in all directions from the point of origin,
dealing 1d8 points of damage +1 point per caster level to nearby living
enemies.

Mass inflict light wounds heals undead in its area rather than damaging
them.
