# Bestow Curse

| Stat             | Value             |
| ---------------- | ----------------- |
| School           | Necromancy        |
| Level            | Acolyte 3, Mage 4 |
| Components       | V, S              |
| Casting Time     | Attack action     |
| Range            | Touch             |
| Target           | Creature touched  |
| Duration         | Permanent         |
| Saving Throw     | Will negates      |
| Spell Resistance | Yes               |

The caster places a curse on the creature touched. The caster chooses
one of the three following effects, depending on the version selected:

–6 penalty to an ability score (minimum score of 1).

–4 penalty on attack rolls, saving throws, ability checks, and skill
checks.

Each turn, the target has a 50% chance to act normally; otherwise, he or
she takes no action.

A character may also invent his or her own curse, but it should be no
more powerful than those described above, and the GM has final say on
the curse’s effect.

The curse cannot be dispelled, but it can be removed with a break
enchantment or remove curse spell.

Bestow curse counters remove curse.
