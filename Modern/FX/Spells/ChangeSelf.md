# Change Self

| Stat         | Value                |
| ------------ | -------------------- |
| School       | Illusion             |
| Level        | Mage 1               |
| Components   | V, S                 |
| Casting Time | Attack action        |
| Range        | Personal             |
| Target       | You                  |
| Duration     | 10 minutes/level (D) |

The caster can make him or herself—including clothing, armor, weapons,
and equipment—look different. The caster can seem 1 foot shorter or
taller, thin, fat, or in between. The caster cannot change body type.
Otherwise, the changes can be as minor or as major as the caster
desires.

The spell does not provide the abilities or mannerisms of the chosen
form. It does not alter the perceived tactile (touch) or audible (sound)
properties of the caster or his or her equipment.

If a character uses this spell to create a disguise, he or she gets a
+10 bonus on the Disguise check.

**Note:** Creatures get a Will save to recognize the illusion if they
interact with it.
