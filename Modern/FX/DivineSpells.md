# Divine Spells

Unlike arcane spells, divine spells draw power from the strength of the
spellcaster’s beliefs. Divine spells tend to be less flashy,
destructive, and disruptive than arcane spells. What they do that arcane
spells don’t do is heal.

## Preparing Divine Spells

The rules for which spells, and how many spells, a divine spellcaster
can cast are given in the class description. The class description also
includes information on how the character prepares spells each day.

## Divine Magical Writings

Divine spells can be written down and deciphered just as arcane spells
can (see Arcane Magical Writings, above). Any character with the
Spellcraft skill can attempt to decipher the divine magical writing and
identify it. However, only characters who are capable of casting the
spell in its divine form can cast a divine spell from a scroll. (The
character does not have to have the spell prepared; he or she only has
to be capable of preparing and casting it in general.)

### 0-Level Acolyte Spells

- [Create Water](Spells/CreateWater.md): Creates 2 gallons/level of pure water.

- [Cure Minor Wounds](Spells/CureMinorWounds.md): Cures 1 point of damage.

- [Detect Magical Aura](Spells/DetectMagicalAura.md): Detects spells, magic items within 60 ft.

- [Inflict Minor Wounds](Spells/InflictMinorWounds.md): Touch attack, 1 point of damage.

- [Light](Spells/Light.md): Object shines like a torch.

- [Read Magic](Spells/ReadMagic.md): Read scrolls and magical writing.

- [Resistance](Spells/Resistance.md): Subject gains +1 on saving throws.

- [Virtue](Spells/Virtue.md): Subject gains 1 temporary hp.

### 1st-level Acolyte Spells

- [Bane](Spells/Bane.md): Enemies suffer –1 attack, –1 on saves against fear.

- [Bless](Spells/Bless.md): Allies gain +1 attack and +1 on saves against fear.

- [Cause Fear](Spells/CauseFear.md): One creature flees for 1d4 rounds.

- [Command](Spells/Command.md): One subject obeys one-word command for 1 round.

- [Comprehend Languages](Spells/ComprehendLanguages.md): Understand all spoken and written languages.

- [Cure Light Wounds](Spells/CureLightWounds.md): Cures 1d8 +1/level damage (max +5).

- [Inflict Light Wounds](Spells/InflictLightWounds.md): Touch, 1d8 +1/level damage (max +5).

- [Magic Weapon](Spells/MagicWeapon.md): Weapon gains +1 bonus.

- [Remove Fear](Spells/RemoveFear.md): +4 on saves against fear for one subject + one additional subject/four levels.

- [Shield of Faith](Spells/ShieldOfFaith.md): Aura grants +2 or higher deflection bonus.

- [Lesser Restoration](Spells/LesserRestoration.md): Dispels magic ability penalty or repairs 1d4 ability damage.

- [Remove Paralysis](Spells/RemoveParalysis.md): Frees one or more creatures from paralysis, hold, or slow.

- [Resist Energy](Spells/ResistEnergy.md): Ignores 10 points of damage/round from one energy type.

- [Shatter](Spells/Shatter.md): Sonic vibration damages objects or crystalline creatures.

- [Silence](Spells/Silence.md): Negates sound in 15-ft: radius.

- [Spider Climb](Spells/SpiderClimb.md): Grants ability to travel on walls and ceilings.

- [Zone of Truth](Spells/ZoneOfTruth.md): Subjects within range cannot lie.
