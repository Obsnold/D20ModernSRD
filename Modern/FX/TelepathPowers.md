# Telepath Powers

## 0-Level Telepath Powers

- [Burst (Dex)](Psionics/Burst.md): Speed improves by 10 feet for 1 round.

- [Daze (Cha)](Psionics/Daze.md): Target loses next action.

- [Detect Psionics (Wis)](Psionics/DetectPsionics.md): Manifester can detect the presence of psionic activity.

- [Distract (Cha)](Psionics/Distract.md): Target’s mind wanders, imparting a –1 penalty on certain actions.

- [Far Hand (Con)](Psionics/FarHand.md): Minor telekinesis.

- [Finger of Fire (Int)](Psionics/FingerOfFire.md): Deal 1d3 fire damage to target.

- [Missive (Cha)](Psionics/Missive.md): Send a one-way telepathic message.

- [Verve (Str)](Psionics/Verve.md): Gain 1 temporary hit point.

## 1st-Level Telepath Powers

- [Attraction (Cha)](Psionics/Attraction.md): Target develops an attraction the manifester specifies.

- [Charm Person (Cha)](Psionics/CharmPerson.md): Makes target manifester’s friend.

- [Control Object (Con)](Psionics/ControlObject.md): Telekinetically animate a small object.

- [Lesser Body Adjustment (Str)](Psionics/LesserBodyAdjustment.md): Heal 1d8 hp, or gain +1 bonus on next Fortitude save to resist poison or disease, or heal 1 point of ability damage.

- [Lesser Mindlink (Cha)](Psionics/LesserMindlink.md): Forge a limited mental bond with target.

- [Object Reading (Wis)](Psionics/ObjectReading.md): Reveal an object’s past.

## 2nd-Level Telepath Powers

- [Brain Lock (Cha)](Psionics/BrainLock.md): Target can’t move or take any mental actions.

- [Clairaudience/Clairvoyance (Wis)](Psionics/ClairaudienceClairvoyance.md): Hear or see at a distance.

- [Detect Thoughts (Cha)](Psionics/DetectThoughts.md): Detect target’s surface thoughts.

- [Inflict Pain (Cha)](Psionics/InflictPain.md): Mental attack deals 3d6 damage to target.

- [Levitate (Dex)](Psionics/Levitate.md): Target moves up or down at manifester’s direction.

- [Sensitivity to Psychic Impressions (Wis)](Psionics/SensitivityToPsychicImpressions.md): Reveal an area’s past.

- [Suggestion (Cha)](Psionics/Suggestion.md): Compels target to follow suggested action.

## 3rd-Level Telepath Powers

- [False Sensory Input (Cha)](Psionics/FalseSensoryInput.md): Falsify one of the target’s senses.

- [Lesser Domination (Cha)](Psionics/LesserDomination.md): Forces target to obey manifester’s will.

- [Mental Blast (Cha)](Psionics/MentalBlast.md): Target stunned for 3d4 rounds.

- [Mindlink (Cha)](Psionics/Mindlink.md): Forge a mental bond with others.

- [Negate Psionics (Con)](Psionics/NegatePsionics.md): Cancels psionic powers and effects.

## 4th-Level Telepath Powers

- [Domination (Cha)](Psionics/Domination.md): Subject obeys manifester’s will.

- [Forced Mindlink (Cha)](Psionics/ForcedMindlink.md): Forge mental bond with unwilling target.

- [Tailor Memory (Cha)](Psionics/TailorMemory.md): Plant false memory in target.

- [Telekinesis (Con)](Psionics/Telekinesis.md): Lift or move 25 pounds per level at long range.

## 5th-Level Telepath Powers

- [Mindprobe (Cha)](Psionics/MindProbe.md): Discover a target’s secret thoughts.

- [Power Resistance (Wis)](Psionics/PowerResistance.md): Target gains power resistance 12.

- [Sending (Dex)](Psionics/Sending.md): Deliver short message anywhere instantly.
