# Poison (Ex)

Poison attacks deal initial damage, such as temporary
ability damage (see above) or some other effect, to the opponent on a
failed Fortitude save. Unless otherwise noted, another saving throw is
required 1 minute later (regardless of the first save’s result) to avoid
secondary damage.

The Fortitude save against poison has a DC equal to 10 + 1/2 poisoning
creature’s HD + poisoning creature’s Constitution modifier (the exact DC
is given in the creature’s Species Traits). A successful save negates
the damage.
