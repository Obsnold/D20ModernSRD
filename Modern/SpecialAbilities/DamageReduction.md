# Damage Reduction (Su)

The creature ignores damage from most weapons
and natural attacks. Wounds heal immediately, or the weapon bounces off
harmlessly (in either case, the opponent knows the attack was
ineffective). The creature takes normal damage from energy attacks (even
nonmagical ones), spells, spell-like abilities, and supernatural
abilities. A magic weapon or a creature with its own damage reduction
can sometimes damage the creature normally, as noted below.

The entry indicates the amount of damage ignored and the type of weapon
that negates the ability.

Any weapon more powerful than the type listed in the note also negates
the ability. A weapon with an enhancement bonus due to magic is
considered more powerful than any weapon that does not have such a
bonus.

For purposes of harming other creatures with damage reduction, a
creature’s natural weapons count as the type that ignores its own innate
damage reduction. However, damage reduction from spells does not confer
this ability. The amount of damage reduction is irrelevant.
