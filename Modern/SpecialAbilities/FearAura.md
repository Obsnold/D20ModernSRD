# Fear Aura (Su)

A fear aura either operates continuously or can be
used at will. In either case, it’s a free action. This ability can
freeze an opponent or cause opponents to become panicked. Other effects
are possible. Negating the fear effect requires a successful Will save
with a DC equal to 10 + 1/2 fearsome creature’s HD + fearsome creature’s
Charisma modifier (the exact DC is given in the creature’s descriptive
text).
