# Blinded

The hero can’t see at all, and thus everything has total concealment to
him or her. The character has a 50% chance to miss in combat.
Furthermore, the blinded character has an effective Dexterity of 3,
along with a –4 penalty on the use of Strength-based and Dexterity-based
skills. This –4 penalty also applies to Search checks and any other
skill checks for which the GM deems sight to be important. The character
can’t make Spot checks or perform any other activity (such as reading)
that requires vision. Heroes who are blind long-term (from birth or
early in life) grow accustomed to these drawbacks and can overcome some
of them (at the GM’s discretion).
