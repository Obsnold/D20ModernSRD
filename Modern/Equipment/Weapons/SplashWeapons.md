# Splash Weapons

Many splash weapons, such as Molotov cocktails, are essentially homemade
devices (improvised explosives). The purchase DC given in Table:
Explosives and Splash Weapons reflects the typical cost of the necessary
components. See the Craft (chemical) skill for details on making
improvised explosives.

## Acid, Mild

A character can throw a flask of acid as a grenadelike weapon. A flask
is made of ceramic, metal, or glass (depending on the substance it has
to hold), with a tight stopper, and holds about 1 pint of liquid. This
entry represents any mild caustic substance. Acid may be purchased in
many places, including hardware stores.

## Molotov Cocktail

A Molotov cocktail is a flask containing a flammable liquid, plugged
with a rag. A Molotov cocktail is easily made by hand (Craft
[chemical] check DC 10 or Intelligence check DC 15). The purchase DC
given is for the components. To use it, the rag must first be lit,
requiring a move action (and a lighter or other source of flame). The
cocktail detonates in 2 rounds or on impact with a solid object,
whichever comes first. A target that takes a direct hit is dealt an
additional 1d6 points of fire damage in the following round and risks
catching on fire.
