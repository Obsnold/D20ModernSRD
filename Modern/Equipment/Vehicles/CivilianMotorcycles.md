# Civilian Motorcycles

Unlike getting into a car, mounting a motorcycle is a free action.
Motorcycles tend to perform better than automobiles, but they provide no
cover to their occupants.

## Ducati 998R

This is a top-of-the-line street bike with a strong heritage of winning
races. The 998R is one square wide and two squares long.

## Harley-Davidson FLSTF Fat Boy

This huge motorcycle sports a 1,450cc engine. It’s designed to look cool
and compete for space on the roads with automobiles. It is one square
wide and two squares long.

## Yamaha YZ250F

A classic dirt bike, this is very similar to the motorcycle used by
United States Army cavalry scouts. The YZ250F is one square wide and two
squares long.
