# Thin Atmosphere

Planets with thin atmospheres have less oxygen per breath than the
standard Earth atmosphere. Many thin atmospheres are the equivalent of
being at a high elevation on Earth, such as on top of a mountain or in
the upper atmosphere. A creature exposed to a thin atmosphere must
succeed on a Fortitude save (DC 20) every hour. On the first failed
save, the creature is fatigued. A fatigued creature that fails a
subsequent save becomes exhausted for as long as it remains in the thin
atmosphere. After 1 hour of complete, uninterrupted rest in a normal
atmosphere, an exhausted creature becomes fatigued. After 8 hours of
complete, uninterrupted rest, a fatigued creature is no longer fatigued.
