# Gravity

The force that gravity exerts on a person determines how they develop
physically as well as their ability to perform certain actions. In
addition, gravity affects the amount of damage a character takes from
falling. Gravity conditions may vary considerably from one environment
to the next. For ease of play these rules* *present four simplified
gravity environments: normal gravity (1.0 g), low gravity (\<1.0 g),
high gravity (\>1.0 g), and zero gravity (0 g). The following sections
summarize the game effects for each type of environment.
