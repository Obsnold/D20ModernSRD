# Thick Atmosphere

Thick atmospheres are those that contain a more dense concentration of
certain elements, like nitrogen, oxygen, or even carbon dioxide, than
the standard Earth atmosphere. These dense atmospheres sometimes contain
a different balance of elements, while others simply contain a higher
number of gas particles in each breath. The effects of exposure to a
thick atmosphere are similar to those of a thin atmosphere (see Thin
Atmosphere, above), except the Fortitude save DC is 15 instead of 20
