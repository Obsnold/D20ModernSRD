# Nonheroic Robots

Most robots exist to perform mundane, routine, or dangerous tasks
without argument or ambition. Except for biodroids and bioreplicas with
heroic class levels, all robots are treated as constructs and share the
general traits outlined below.

Nonheroic robots don’t get class levels. However, they can receive
factory-installed skill and feat software, allowing them to emulate
specific skills and feats as part of their programming (see Skill
Software and Feat Software).
