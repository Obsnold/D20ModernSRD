# Robots as Heroes

This section presents two types of anthropomorphic robots that can be
played as heroes: the *biodroid *(more commonly referred to as an
“android”) and the *bioreplica *(also known as a “synthetic”). Although
neither is truly sentient, their artificial intelligence allows them to
make independent decisions and learn through experience. Biodroids are
present in societies with technology of Progress Level 6 or higher,
while bioreplicas are products of Progress Level 7 ingenuity.

A biodroid or bioreplica hero begins play as a 1st-level Strong, Fast,
Tough, Smart, Dedicated, or Charismatic hero. Whereas other robots may
only advance as Ordinary characters, biodroid and bioreplica heroes gain
levels just as human characters do. They may even choose to take levels
in advanced classes for which they’re eligible. There are advantages and
drawbacks to playing a biodroid or bioreplica hero; these are discussed
in-depth below.
