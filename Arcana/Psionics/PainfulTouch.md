# Painful Touch

| Stat               | Value             |
| ------------------ | ----------------- |
| Key Ability        | Strength          |
| Level              | Battle Mind 2     |
| Display            | Material, Visual  |
| Manifestation Time | Attack action     |
| Range              | Personal          |
| Target             | You               |
| Duration           | 1 round/level (D) |
| Power Point Cost   | 3                 |

Your unarmed attacks cause additional pain. When you make a successful
unarmed attack that deals damage (or in conjunction with any bite or
claw powers), you deal an additional 1d6 points of nonlethal damage to
the target. The total amount of damage dealt is used to determine the
effects of massive damage.
