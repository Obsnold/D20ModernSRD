# Telepath Powers

The Telepath chooses psionic powers from the following list.

## 0-Level Telepath Powers

- [Burst (Dex)](../../Modern/FX/Psionics/Burst.md): Speed improves by 10 feet for 1 round.

- [Daze (Cha)](../../Modern/FX/Psionics/Daze.md): Target loses next action.

- [Detect Psionics (Wis)](../../Modern/FX/Psionics/DetectPsionics.md): You detect the presence of psionics activity.

- [Distract (Cha)](../../Modern/FX/Psionics/Distract.md): Target’s mind wanders, imparting a –1 penalty on certain actions.

- [Far Hand (Con)](../../Modern/FX/Psionics/FarHand.md): Minor telekinesis.

- [Finger of Fire (Int)](../../Modern/FX/Psionics/FingerOfFire.md): You deal 1d3 points of fire damage to one target.

- [Missive (Cha)](../../Modern/FX/Psionics/Missive.md): Send a one-way telepathic message.

- [Telempathic Projection (Cha)](TelempathicProjection.md): Modify the subject’s emotions.

- [Verve (Str)](../../Modern/FX/Psionics/Verve.md): Gain 1 temporary hit point.

## 1st-Level Telepath Powers

- [Attraction (Cha)](../../Modern/FX/Psionics/Attraction.md): Target develops an attraction you specify.

- [Charm Person (Cha)](../../Modern/FX/Psionics/CharmPerson.md): Makes target your friend.

- [Conceal Thoughts (Cha)](ConcealThoughts.md): Hide your motives.

- [Control Object (Con)](../../Modern/FX/Psionics/ControlObject.md): Telekinetically animate a small object.

- [Lesser Body Adjustment (Str)](../../Modern/FX/Psionics/LesserBodyAdjustment.md): Heal 1d8 hp, or gain +1 bonus on next Fortitude save to resist poison or disease, or heal 1 point of ability damage.

- [Lesser Mindlink (Cha)](../../Modern/FX/Psionics/LesserMindlink.md): Forge a limited mental bond with target.

- [Object Reading (Wis)](../../Modern/FX/Psionics/ObjectReading.md): Reveal an object’s past.

- [White Noise (Con)](WhiteNoise.md): Scramble electronic transmissions.

## 2nd-Level Telepath Powers

- [Brain Lock (Cha)](../../Modern/FX/Psionics/BrainLock.md): Target can’t move or take any mental actions.

- [Clairaudience/Clairvoyance (Wis)](../../Modern/FX/Psionics/ClairaudienceClairvoyance.md): Hear or see at a distance.

- [Detect Thoughts (Cha)](../../Modern/FX/Psionics/DetectThoughts.md): Detect target’s surface thoughts.

- [Electronic Fog (Con)](ElectronicFog.md): Scramble electronic signals.

- [Inflict Pain (Cha)](../../Modern/FX/Psionics/InflictPain.md): Mental attack deals 3d6 points of damage to target.

- [Levitate (Dex)](../../Modern/FX/Psionics/Levitate.md): Target moves up or down at your direction.

- [Sensitivity to Psychic Impressions (Wis)](../../Modern/FX/Psionics/SensitivityToPsychicImpressions.md): Reveal an area’s past.

- [Suggestion (Cha)](../../Modern/FX/Psionics/Suggestion.md): Compels target to follow suggested action.

## 3rd-Level Telepath Powers

- [Charm Creature (Cha)](CharmCreature.md): Make creature belief you to be an ally.

- [False Sensory Input (Cha)](../../Modern/FX/Psionics/FalseSensoryInput.md): Falsify one of the target’s senses.

- [Lesser Domination (Cha)](../../Modern/FX/Psionics/LesserDomination.md): Forces target to obey your will.

- [Mental Blast (Cha)](../../Modern/FX/Psionics/MentalBlast.md): Target stunned for 3d4 rounds.

- [Mindlink (Cha)](../../Modern/FX/Psionics/Mindlink.md): Forge a mental bond with others.

- [Negate Psionics (Con)](../../Modern/FX/Psionics/NegatePsionics.md): Cancels psionic powers and effects.

## 4th-Level Telepath Powers

- [Domination (Cha)](../../Modern/FX/Psionics/Domination.md): Subject obeys your will.

- [Forced Mindlink (Cha)](../../Modern/FX/Psionics/ForcedMindlink.md): Forge mental bond with unwilling target.

- [Inertial Barrier (Con)](InertialBarrier.md): Subject gains DR 10/—.

- [Mindwipe (Cha)](Mindwipe.md): Subject’s recent experiences are wiped away.

- [Tailor Memory (Cha)](../../Modern/FX/Psionics/TailorMemory.md): Plant false memory in target.

- [Telekinesis (Con)](../../Modern/FX/Psionics/Telekinesis.md): Lift or move 25 pounds per level at long range.

## 5th-Level Telepath Powers

- [Catapsi (Cha)](Catapsi.md): Reduce power manifestation of others.

- [Mindprobe (Cha)](../../Modern/FX/Psionics/MindProbe.md): Discover a target’s secret thoughts.

- [Power Resistance (Wis)](../../Modern/FX/Psionics/PowerResistance.md): Target gains power resistance 12.

- [Sending (Dex)](../../Modern/FX/Psionics/Sending.md): Deliver short message anywhere instantly.

- [Signal Feed (Cha)](SignalFeed.md): Cameras show what you want them to show.
