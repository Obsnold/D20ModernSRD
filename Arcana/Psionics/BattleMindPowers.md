# Battle Mind Powers

The Battle Mind chooses psionic powers from the following list.

## 0-Level Battle Mind Powers

- [Burst (Dex)](../../Modern/FX/Psionics/Burst.md): Speed improves by 10 feet for 1 round.

- [Detect Psionics (Wis)](../../Modern/FX/Psionics/DetectPsionics.md): You detect the presence of psionics activity.

- [Far Punch (Con)](../../Modern/FX/Psionics/FarPunch.md): Telekinetic strike deals 1 damage.

- [Finger of Fire (Int)](../../Modern/FX/Psionics/FingerOfFire.md): Deal 1d3 points of fire damage to target.

- [Lesser Natural Armor (Str)](LesserNaturalArmor.md): Gain +1 natural armor bonus to Defense.

- [Valor (Str)](../../Modern/FX/Psionics/Valor.md): Gain a +1 morale bonus on saving throws.

- [Verve (Str)](../../Modern/FX/Psionics/Verve.md): Gain 1 temporary hit point.

## 1st-Level Battle Mind Powers

- [Biofeedback (Str)](Biofeedback.md): Some damage taken as nonlethal.

- [Combat Precognition (Wis)](../../Modern/FX/Psionics/CombatPrecognition.md): Gain a +1 insight bonus to Defense.

- [Fire Bolt (Int)](../../Modern/FX/Psionics/FireBolt.md): Deals 1d6+1 points of fire damage to target.

- [Lesser Bioweapon (Str)](../../Modern/FX/Psionics/LesserBioweapon.md): Create a staff of bioenergy that deals 1d4 points of bludgeoning damage.

- [Lesser Concussion (Con)](../../Modern/FX/Psionics/LesserConcussion.md): Mentally pummel target for 1d6 points of damage.

- [Vigor (Str)](../../Modern/FX/Psionics/Vigor.md): Gain 3 temporary hit points.

- [White Noise (Con)](WhiteNoise.md): Scramble electronic transmissions.

## 2nd-Level Battle Mind Powers

- [Claws of the Bear (Str)](../../Modern/FX/Psionics/ClawsOfTheBear.md): Your claw attack deals 1d12 points of damage.

- [Combat Focus (Wis)](../../Modern/FX/Psionics/CombatFocus.md): Gain a +4 insight bonus on initiative checks.

- [Combat Prescience (Wis)](../../Modern/FX/Psionics/CombatPrescience.md): Gain a +2 insight bonus on attack rolls.

- [Concussion (Con)](../../Modern/FX/Psionics/Concussion.md): Mentally pummel target for 3d6 points of damage.

- [Darkvision (Wis)](../../Modern/FX/Psionics/Darkvision.md): See in the dark.

- [Electric Charge (Int)](../../Modern/FX/Psionics/ElectricCharge.md): Shocking touch deals 2d6 points of damage to target.

- [Painful Touch (Str)](PainfulTouch.md): Unarmed attack deals 1d6 points of additional nonlethal damage.

## 3rd-Level Battle Mind Powers

- [Bite of the Tiger (Str)](BiteOfTheTiger.md): Bite for 2d8 points of damage.

- [Lightning Strike (Int)](../../Modern/FX/Psionics/LightningStrike.md): Deals 3d6 points of electrical damage in a 30-foot radius.

- [Improved Biofeedback (Str)](ImprovedBiofeedback.md): Larger amount of damage taken as nonlethal damage.

- [Metaphysical Weapon (Int)](../../Modern/FX/Psionics/MetaphysicalWeapon.md): Weapon gains a +3 enhancement bonus.

- [Mind Darts (Int)](../../Modern/FX/Psionics/MindDarts.md): A flurry of mental bursts deals 2d6 points of damage to target.

- [Negate Psionics (Con)](../../Modern/FX/Psionics/NegatePsionics.md): Cancels psionic powers and effects.

- [Whitefire (Int)](../../Modern/FX/Psionics/Whitefire.md): Deals 5d4 points of fire damage in 20-foot radius.

## 4th-Level Battle Mind Powers

- [Fire Storm (Int)](../../Modern/FX/Psionics/FireStorm.md): Deals 5d6 points of fire damage in 30-foot radius.

- [Greater Bioweapon (Str)](../../Modern/FX/Psionics/GreaterBioweapon.md): Creates a staff of bioenergy that deals 2d8 points of bludgeoning damage.

- [Natural Armor (Str)](../../Modern/FX/Psionics/NaturalArmor.md): You gain a +4 natural armor bonus to Defense.

- [Psychofeedback (Str)](../../Modern/FX/Psionics/Psychofeedback.md): Use power points to boost Str, Dex, and/or Con modifiers.

- [Short Sharp Shock (Con)](ShortSharpShock.md): Deal 2d10 points of nonlethal damage, and destroy electronic records.
