# Psionic Agent Powers

The Psionic Agent chooses psionic powers from the following list.

## 0-Level Psionic Agent Powers

- [Burst (Dex)](../../Modern/FX/Psionics/Burst.md): Speed improves by 10 feet for 1 round.

- [Detect Poison (Wis)](DetectPoison.md): Detect the presence of poisons.

- [Detect Psionics (Wis)](../../Modern/FX/Psionics/DetectPsionics.md): Detect the presence of psionics activity.

- [Far Punch (Con)](../../Modern/FX/Psionics/FarPunch.md): Telekinetic strike deals 1 point of damage.

- [Finger of Fire (Int)](../../Modern/FX/Psionics/FingerOfFire.md): Deal 1d3 points of fire damage to target.

- [Missive (Cha)](../../Modern/FX/Psionics/Missive.md): Send a one-way telepathic message.

- [Verve (Str)](../../Modern/FX/Psionics/Verve.md): Gain 1 temporary hit point.

## 1st-Level Psionic Agent Powers

- [Call Weaponry (Dex)](CallWeaponry.md): Summon weapon to your hand.

- [Combat Precognition (Wis)](../../Modern/FX/Psionics/CombatPrecognition.md): Gain a +1 insight bonus to Defense.

- [Feather Fall (Dex)](FeatherFall.md): Objects or creatures fall slowly.

- [Identify (Wis)](Identify.md): Identify single feature of magical or psionics item.

- [Lesser Concussion (Con)](../../Modern/FX/Psionics/LesserConcussion.md): Mentally pummel target for 1d6 points of damage.

- [Object Reading (Wis)](../../Modern/FX/Psionics/ObjectReading.md): Reveal an object’s past.

- [Spider Climb (Dex)](SpiderClimb.md): Walk on walls and ceilings.

## 2nd-Level Psionic Agent Powers

- [Chameleon (Str)](Chameleon.md): Gain +10 bonus on Hide checks.

- [Combat Focus (Wis)](../../Modern/FX/Psionics/CombatFocus.md): Gain a +4 insight bonus on initiative checks.

- [Combat Prescience (Wis)](../../Modern/FX/Psionics/CombatPrescience.md): Gain a +2 insight bonus on attack rolls.

- [Darkvision (Wis)](../../Modern/FX/Psionics/Darkvision.md): See in the dark.

- [Knock (Dex)](Knock.md): Open locked doors.

- [Levitate (Dex)](../../Modern/FX/Psionics/Levitate.md): Target moves up or down at your direction.

- [Sensitivity to Psychic Impressions (Wis)](../../Modern/FX/Psionics/SensitivityToPsychicImpressions.md): Reveal an area’s past.

## 3rd-Level Psionic Agent Powers

- [Dimension Slide (Dex)](DimensionSlide.md): Move to spot within range you can see.

- [Fly (Dex)](Fly.md): Subject flies at speed of 90 ft.

- [Instant Reload (Dex)](InstantReload.md): Reload your weapon automatically.

- [Invisibility Purge (Wis)](InvisibilityPurge.md): Dispels invisibility within 5 ft./ level.

- [Metaphysical Weapon (Int)](../../Modern/FX/Psionics/MetaphysicalWeapon.md): Weapon gains a +3 enhancement bonus.

- [Prowess (Wis)](Prowess.md): Take an extra attack of opportunity.

- [Ubiquitous Vision (Wis)](UbiquitousVision.md): You have all-around vision.

## 4th-Level Psionic Agent Powers

- [Dimension Door (Dex)](DimensionDoor.md): Teleports you and up to 500 lb. a short distance.

- [Fate of One (Wis)](FateOfOne.md): Reroll a failed roll.

- [Freedom of Movement (Dex)](FreedomOfMovement.md): Move normally despite impediments.

- [Ghost Shot (Dex)](GhostShot.md): Create intangible bullets.

- [Immovability (Str)](Immovability.md): Become impossible to move.

- [Steadfast Perception (Wis)](SteadfastPerception.md): +4 bonus against illusions, +2 bonus on Search and Spot checks.
