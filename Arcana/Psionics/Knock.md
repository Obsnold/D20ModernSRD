# Knock

| Stat               | Value                                                          |
|---|---|
| Key Ability        | Dexterity                                                      |
| Level              | Telepath 2                                                     |
| Display            | Material                                                       |
| Manifestation Time | Attack action                                                  |
| Range              | Medium (100 ft. + 10 ft./level)                                |
| Target             | One door, box, or chest with an area of up to 10 sq. ft./level |
| Duration           | Instantaneous (see text)                                       |
| Saving Throw       | None                                                           |
| Power Resistance   | No                                                             |
| Power Point Cost   | 3                                                              |

The *knock* power opens stuck, barred, locked, or mechanically or
electronically sealed doors, as well at those with hidden mechanisms. It
also loosens welds, shackles, or chains (provided they serve to hold
closures shut). In all other cases, the door does not relock itself or
becomes stuck again on its own. *Knock* does not raise barred gates or
similar impediments, nor does it affect ropes, vines, and the like. Note
that the effect is limited by the area. Each power can undo up to two
means of preventing egress through a portal.
