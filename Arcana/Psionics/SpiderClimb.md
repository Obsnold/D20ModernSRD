# Spider Climb

| Stat | Value |
|---|---|
| Key Ability | Dexterity |
| Level | Telepath 1 |
| Display | Material |
| Manifestation Time | Attack action |
| Range | Touch |
| Target | Creature touched |
| Duration | 10 minutes/level |
| Saving Throw | Will negates (harmless) |
| Power Resistance | Yes (harmless) |
| Power Point Cost | 1 |

The subject can climb and travel on vertical surfaces or even traverse
ceilings as well as a spider does. The affected creature must have bare
hands and feet to climb in this manner. The subject climbs at half its
speed. A creature with a Strength score of at least 20 +1 per manifester
level can pull the subject off a wall.
