# Divine Spells

The following spells may be cast by divine spellcasters.

Spells printed in **bold** text can be found under [Arcana Spells](spells.md).

Spells printed in ***bold italic*** text can be found in [Modern Spells](../../Modern/FX/Spells/spells.md).

## 0-level Divine Spells

- [***Create Water.***](../../Modern/FX/Spells/CreateWater.md) Creates 2 gallons/level of pure water.

- [***Cure Minor Wounds.***](../../Modern/FX/Spells/CureMinorWounds.md) Cures 1 point of damage.

- [***Detect Magical Aura.***](../../Modern/FX/Spells/DetectMagicalAura.md) Detects spells, magic items within 60 feet.

- [**Haywire.**](Haywire.md) Causes a single device to behave randomly and erratically.

- [***Inflict Minor Wounds.***](../../Modern/FX/Spells/InflictMinorWounds.md) Touch attack, 1 point of damage.

- [***Light.***](../../Modern/FX/Spells/Light.md) Object shines like a torch.

- [**Mending.**](Mending.md) Repairs small breaks or tears in objects.

- [***Read Magic.***](../../Modern/FX/Spells/ReadMagic.md) Read scrolls and magical writing.

- [***Resistance.***](../../Modern/FX/Spells/Resistance.md) Subject gains +1 on saving throws.

- [***Virtue.***](../../Modern/FX/Spells/Virtue.md) Subject gains 1 temporary hp.

## 1st-level Divine Spells

- [***Bane.***](../../Modern/FX/Spells/Bane.md) Enemies suffer –1 attack, –1 on saves against fear.

- [***Bless.***](../../Modern/FX/Spells/Bless.md) Allies gain +1 attack and +1 on saves against fear.

- [***Cause Fear.***](../../Modern/FX/Spells/CauseFear.md) One creature flees for 1d4 rounds.

- [**Clean.**](Clean.md) Eliminates grime, dirt, and bacterial contaminants from the target, and renders target scentless.

- [***Command.***](../../Modern/FX/Spells/Command.md) One subject obeys one-word command for 1 round.

- [***Comprehend Languages.***](../../Modern/FX/Spells/ComprehendLanguages.md) Understand all spoken and written languages.

- [***Cure Light Wounds.***](../../Modern/FX/Spells/CureLightWounds.md) Cures 1d8 +1/level damage (max +5).

- [**Degauss.**](Degauss.md) Erases a single device that contains electronic data.

- [**Faerie Fire.**](FaerieFire.md) Pale glow surrounds and outlines creatures and objects in a 5-foot-radius burst.

- [***Inflict Light Wounds.***](../../Modern/FX/Spells/InflictLightWounds.md) Touch, 1d8 +1/level damage (max +5).

- [**Instant Identify.**](InstantIdentify.md) Determines all magic properties of a single magic item.

- [***Magic Weapon.***](../../Modern/FX/Spells/MagicWeapon.md) Weapon gains +1 bonus.

- [**Obscuring Mist.**](ObscuringMist.md) Creates cloud of vapor that obscures all sight, including darkvision, beyond 5 feet.

- [***Remove Fear.***](../../Modern/FX/Spells/RemoveFear.md) +4 on saves against fear for one subject + one additional subject/four levels.

- [**Sanctuary.**](Sanctuary.md) Opponents can’t attack you, and you can’t attack them.

- [***Shield of Faith.***](../../Modern/FX/Spells/ShieldOfFaith.md) Aura grants +2 or higher deflection bonus.

- [**Summon Vivilor I.**](SummonVivilorI.md) Summons a 1st-level vivilor from Shadow to fight for you.

- [**Trace Purge.**](TracePurge.md) Removes physical traces of the subject’s presence or passage.

## 2nd-level Divine Spells

- [***Aid.***](../../Modern/FX/Spells/Aid.md) +1 attack, +1 on saves against fear, 1d8 temporary hit points.

- [***Augury.***](../../Modern/FX/Spells/Augury.md) Learn whether an action will be good or bad.

- [***Cure Moderate Wounds.***](../../Modern/FX/Spells/CureModerateWounds.md) Cures 2d8 +1/level damage (max +10).

- [**Darkness.**](Darkness.md) Causes an object to radiate darkness out to a 20-foot radius.

- [**Dataread.**](Dataread.md) You can access and read data stored in any machine-readable data source.

- [**Daylight.**](Daylight.md) Object touched sheds light as bright as full daylight in a 60-foot radius.

- [***Delay Poison.***](../../Modern/FX/Spells/DelayPoison.md) Stops poison from harming subject for 1 hour/level.

- [***Enhance Ability.***](../../Modern/FX/Spells/EnhanceAbility.md) Subject gains +5 bonus to one ability score for 1 min./level.

- [***Hold Person.***](../../Modern/FX/Spells/HoldPerson.md) Holds one person helpless; 1 round/level.

- [***Inflict Moderate Wounds.***](../../Modern/FX/Spells/InflictModerateWounds.md) Touch attack, 2d8 +1/level damage (max +10).

- [***Lesser Restoration.***](../../Modern/FX/Spells/LesserRestoration.md) Dispels magic ability penalty or repairs 1d4 ability damage.

- [***Remove Paralysis.***](../../Modern/FX/Spells/RemoveParalysis.md) Frees one or more creatures from paralysis, hold, or slow.

- [***Resist Energy.***](../../Modern/FX/Spells/ResistEnergy.md) Ignores 10 points of damage/round from one energy type.

- [**Shatter.**](Shatter.md) Sonic vibration damages objects or crystalline creatures.

- [**Shield Other.**](ShieldOther.md) You take half of the subject’s damage. Subject gains +1 deflection bonus to Defense and +1 resistance bonus to saves.

- [***Silence.***](../../Modern/FX/Spells/Silence.md) Negates sound in 15-foot radius.

- [**Speak with Animals.**](SpeakWithAnimals.md) You can comprehend and communicate with animals.

- [***Spider Climb.***](../../Modern/FX/Spells/SpiderClimb.md) Grants ability to travel on walls and ceilings.

- [**Summon Vivilor II.**](SummonVivilorII.md) Summons a 2nd-level vivilor or 1d3 1st-level vivilors to fight for you.

- [***Zone of Truth.***](../../Modern/FX/Spells/ZoneOfTruth.md) Subjects within range cannot lie.

## 3rd-level Divine Spells

- [***Animate Dead.***](../../Modern/FX/Spells/AnimateDead.md) Creates undead skeletons and zombies.

- [***Bestow Curse.***](../../Modern/FX/Spells/BestowCurse.md) –6 to an ability; –4 on attacks, saves, and checks; or 50% chance of losing each action.

- [***Cure Serious Wounds.***](../../Modern/FX/Spells/CureSeriousWounds.md) Cures 3d8 +1/level damage (max +10).

- [***Dispel Magic.***](../../Modern/FX/Spells/DispelMagic.md) Cancels magical spells and effects.

- [***Glyph of Warding.***](../../Modern/FX/Spells/GlyphOfWarding.md) Inscription harms those who pass it.

- [***Inflict Serious Wounds.***](../../Modern/FX/Spells/InflictSeriousWounds.md) Touch attack, 3d8 +1/level damage (max +10).

- [***Locate Object.***](../../Modern/FX/Spells/LocateObject.md) Senses direction toward object (specific or type).

- [**Magic Circle.**](MagicCircle.md) 10-foot-radius area grants +2 bonus to Defense and saves, counters mind control, and hedges out summoned and conjured creatures of specified allegiance.

- [***Prayer.***](../../Modern/FX/Spells/Prayer.md) Allies gain +1 on most rolls, and enemies suffer –1.

- [**Recharge.**](Recharge.md) Removes debilitating conditions and grants a +4 bonus on Fortitude saves against poison for 1 minute.

- [***Remove Curse.***](../../Modern/FX/Spells/RemoveCurse.md) Frees object or person from curse.

- [***Remove Disease.***](../../Modern/FX/Spells/RemoveDisease.md) Cures all diseases affecting subject.

- [***Searing Light.***](../../Modern/FX/Spells/SearingLight.md) Ray deals 1d8/two levels, more against undead.

- [**Secret Pocket.**](SecretPocket.md) Creates extradimensional space within a pocket on a garment.

- [**Shadowmoth Plague.**](ShadowmothPlague.md) Summons a cloud of shadowmoths that fills a 5-foot-high, 10-foot-radius spread.

- [**Shutdown.**](Shutdown.md) Electric devices in area cease to function for the duration of the spell.

- [***Status.***](../../Modern/FX/Spells/Status.md) Monitors condition and position of one ally per 3 caster levels.

- [**Summon Vivilor III.**](SummonVivilorIII.md) Summons a 3rd-level vivilor, 1d3 2nd-level vivilors, or 1d4+1 1st-level vivilors to fight for you.

- [**Vampiric Touch.**](VampiricTouch.md) Touch deals 1d6 points of damage per two caster levels; caster gains damage as temporary hit points.

- [***Water Breathing.***](../../Modern/FX/Spells/WaterBreathing.md) Subjects can breathe underwater.

## 4th-level Divine Spells

- [**Crawling Carpet.**](CrawlingCarpet.md) Summons a 10-foot-radius carpet of monstrous centipedes, scarab beetles, or spiders.

- [***Cure Critical Wounds.***](../../Modern/FX/Spells/CureCriticalWounds.md) Cures 4d8 +1/level damage (max +10).

- [***Discern Lies.***](../../Modern/FX/Spells/DiscernLies.md) Reveals deliberate falsehoods.

- [**Divination.**](Divination.md) Provides useful (if cryptic) advice to a single question.

- [***Faith’s Fury.***](../../Modern/FX/Spells/FaithSFury.md) Damages and blinds creatures with a specific allegiance.

- [***Freedom of Movement.***](../../Modern/FX/Spells/FreedomOfMovement.md) Subject moves normally despite impediments.

- [***Greater Magic Weapon.***](../../Modern/FX/Spells/GreaterMagicWeapon.md) +1 bonus/three levels (max +5).

- [***Inflict Critical Wounds.***](../../Modern/FX/Spells/InflictCriticalWounds.md) Touch attack, 4d8 +1/level damage (max +10).

- [**Magic Bullets.**](MagicBullets.md) Enchants bullets with the power to *cure light wounds* or *inflict light wounds*.

- [***Neutralize Poison.***](../../Modern/FX/Spells/NeutralizePoison.md) Detoxifies venom in or on subject.

- [***Restoration.***](../../Modern/FX/Spells/Restoration.md) Restores level and ability score drains.

- [**Spell Immunity.**](SpellImmunity.md) Subject is immune to one spell per four levels.

- [**Summon Vivilor IV.**](SummonVivilorIV.md) Summons a 4th-level vivilor, 1d3 3rd-level vivilors, or 1d4+1 2nd-level vivilors to fight for you.

- [***Tongues.***](../../Modern/FX/Spells/Tongues.md) Speak any language.

- [**Via Negativa.**](ViaNegativa.md) Crackling energy deals 4d6 points of damage per round to grappled foe; +5 bonus to grapple checks.

- [**Wall Walk.**](WallWalk.md) Subject can move on ceilings and walls at normal speed as well as hold and wield weapons.

## 5th-level Divine Spells

- [***Break Enchantment.***](../../Modern/FX/Spells/BreakEnchantment.md) Frees subjects from enchantments, alterations, curses, and petrification.

- [***Flaming Wrath.***](../../Modern/FX/Spells/FlamingWrath.md) Smites foes with fire (1d6/level).

- [***Greater Command.***](../../Modern/FX/Spells/GreaterCommand.md) As *command*, but affects one subject/level.

- [***Insect Plague.***](../../Modern/FX/Spells/InsectPlague.md) Insect horde limits vision, inflicts damage, and weak creatures flee.

- [***Mass Cure Light Wounds.***](../../Modern/FX/Spells/MassCureLightWounds.md) Cures 1d8 +1/level damage for many creatures.

- [***Mass Inflict Light Wounds.***](../../Modern/FX/Spells/MassInflictLightWounds.md) Deals 1d8 +1/level damage to many creatures.

- [**Rage of Bees.**](RageOfBees.md) Summons 1d3 swarms of sentient killer bees to fight for you.

- [***Raise Dead.***](../../Modern/FX/Spells/RaiseDead.md) Restores life to subject who died up to 1 day/level ago.

- [**Scrying.**](Scrying.md) Spies on subject from a distance.

- [**Spell Resistance.**](SpellResistance.md) Subject gains spell resistance 12 + your caster level.

- [**Summon Vivilor V.**](SummonVivilorV.md) Summons a 5th-level vivilor, 1d3 4th-level vivilors, or 1d4+1 3rd-level vivilors to fight for you.

- [**Synchronicity.**](Synchronicity.md) Subject isn’t delayed by the inconveniences of modern life.

- [***True Seeing.***](../../Modern/FX/Spells/TrueSeeing.md) See all things as they really are.

- [***Wall of Stone.***](../../Modern/FX/Spells/WallOfStone.md) Creates a stone wall that can be shaped.
